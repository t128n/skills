# Decision memo

State the decision required, relevant context and constraints, viable alternatives, and their meaningful trade-offs and risks. Compare alternatives fairly against the actual decision criteria, including the status quo if viable. Give a recommendation and rationale only if supported; otherwise explain what evidence or approval would enable a decision, or make the recommendation explicitly conditional.

Identify material open questions, decision makers, and approval requirements only when known. A memo evaluates or proposes a decision; it does not imply approval. For architectural decisions, follow existing ADR or RFC conventions and link existing records rather than creating a competing source of truth.
