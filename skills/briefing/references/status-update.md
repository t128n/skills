# Status update

Use Progress, Plans, Problems (3P) by default, adapting to the project's reporting convention. Describe meaningful outcomes and changes since the prior update, planned work for the relevant period, and current problems, blockers, or risks. Include decisions or help needed when material. Preserve the stated reporting period and distinguish plans from commitments.

Avoid activity logs and filler. Do not invent percentages, dates, RAG indicators, or overall status. Use a structured indicator only when the source provides a defensible basis, and explain qualifications when needed.
