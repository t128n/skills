# Handoff note

Prioritize the context the recipient cannot reliably reconstruct: purpose and current state, relevant architecture or operations, completed and outstanding work, known issues and blockers, decisions and supported rationale, and next actions. Preserve important interfaces, dependencies, verification information, ownership, and operational caveats when available. Link critical authoritative documents, issues, and runbooks rather than copying them wholesale.

Distinguish what is complete, planned, uncertain, and merely recommended. Do not imply a transfer of ownership, access, or responsibility has occurred unless the source confirms it.
