# Decisions

Record a decision as confirmed only when the source explicitly establishes agreement or approval. A preference, proposal, recommendation, or lack of objection is not itself a confirmed outcome. If approval remains outstanding, say so. Capture rationale, scope, conditions, decision owner, and approver only when supported.

Clearly label a deferred decision, and identify a superseded decision or its replacement only when the evidence establishes that relationship. If sources conflict about whether a decision was made, retain both claims and identify the unresolved discrepancy. Do not infer that a later comment automatically supersedes an earlier outcome.

Link to existing ADRs, RFCs, or issues when available; avoid making meeting notes a competing authoritative decision record. Significant architecture decisions may merit a separate ADR under the project's conventions, but creating meeting notes does not authorize generating one.
