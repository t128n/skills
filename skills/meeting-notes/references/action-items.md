# Action items

Record a confirmed follow-up as an action or deliverable, with the responsible owner, deadline, dependencies, blockers, completion criteria, and status only where the source supports them. Distinguish an explicitly assigned but ownerless action from a suggested action; mark missing owner as `Unassigned` and missing due date as `Not specified` when these fields are displayed. Do not guess dates from relative phrases without a reliable reference date.

Keep suggested follow-ups labeled as suggestions, not commitments; a statement that someone should probably do something is not proof of acceptance or assignment. Record a follow-up question as a question, not as an assigned task. An agreed task with no identified owner is unassigned work. Do not create tickets or notify anyone unless specifically requested.
