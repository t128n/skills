# Meeting minutes

Working meeting notes prioritize decisions and outcomes, confirmed follow-ups, and unresolved questions. Summarize relevant context by topic instead of reproducing the order of conversation. A brief meeting without decisions or actions may need only a concise summary; a workshop with unrelated topics may need distinct topic sections with their respective outcomes. Include risks or blockers when material.

Include date, participants, purpose, and related documents only when known and useful. Use neutral, factual language; attribute a view when its speaker affects interpretation. Preserve uncertainty, conditions, and discrepancies. Use timestamps or source links when they help readers verify a consequential statement.

These are working notes, not a substitute for an organization's formal or legally required minutes. If formal minutes are requested, follow the applicable governing rules and project conventions rather than assuming discussion summaries belong in the official record.
